# Kartik Service Centre (ಕಾರ್ತಿಕ ಸರ್ವಿಸ್ ಸೆಂಟರ್)

Modern, futuristic 3D showcase website for **Kartik Service Centre** — an automotive workshop in Chitguppa, Karnataka specializing in two-wheeler mechanical care, precision engine overhauls, high-pressure foam washing, and emergency puncture repairs.

## 🚀 Features
- **3D Interactive Machinery Showcase:** Procedural Three.js 3D models for garage machinery (Metro Super Compressor, Triplex Plunger Washer, Pneumatic Air Gun, Power Drill, Angle Grinder, Rapid Multi-Stage Battery Charger) with full 360° orbit inspection studio.
- **15 Floating 3D Metal Workshop Tools:** Realistic metallic Three.js PBR models (Hathoda, Pakkad, Pana, Pechkas, L-Key, Chheni, Spark Plug, etc.) floating along viewport side margins.
- **Horizontal Workshop Gallery:** Carousel scroll and 16-photo grid toggle with high-resolution image zoom lightbox.
- **Ultra-Clean Light Theme:** Porcelain canvas (`#f8fafc`), clean slate cards, and pastel accent badges.
- **Direct Floating Contact:** Floating dialer and official WhatsApp dock buttons directly linking to Kartik (`6363176145`).
- **Authentic Workshop Photography:** Real storefront imagery, outdoor workshop board, and printed business card from Chitguppa.
